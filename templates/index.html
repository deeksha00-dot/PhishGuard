    About PhishGuard

    How it works

    PhishGuard is a rule-based detector, not a trained AI model. It looks for urgency, requests for OTPs or passwords, prize and refund scams, secrecy, risky attachments and suspicious link structure. Each finding adds a fixed number of points, each category has a ceiling, and the total is shown as a score from 0 to 100.

    Low 0-24Medium 25-49High 50-74Critical 75-100

    Links are inspected as text only. PhishGuard never opens them, so it cannot judge a site's live reputation or content.

    Stay safe

    • Banks and companies never ask for your OTP, PIN or password.
    • Go to the official website yourself instead of using a link in a message.
    • Pressure and secrecy are classic scam tactics. Pause and verify.
    • Never pay a fee to receive a prize, refund or parcel.

    Disclaimer

    The risk score is a heuristic estimate, not a verified probability that something is malicious. A low score does not prove a message is safe, and a high score does not prove it is a scam. Treat PhishGuard as a demonstration tool and always verify through official channels. Do not paste real passwords or OTPs.

    PhishGuard is a college hackathon prototype. Detection is rule-based and runs entirely on this machine.
